- The EVM side of the kernel now carries the wei amounts it authors as `Wei`:
  the EVM ledger's credit and debit, the credits written from outside a
  transaction, the EVM leg of the cross-runtime amount header, bridge
  deposits, the EVM fee amounts, the prepay check and the simulation funding.
  The EVM execution itself keeps its own integer type.
- The kernel's copies of the wei/mutez scale factor derive from
  `ONE_MUTEZ_WEI`, and the tests of the predeployed contracts take their
  expected values from it.
- The untyped `tezos_ethereum::wei::gas_from_wei` and `ceil_div` are removed
  in favour of the typed `tezosx_types::wei_to_evm_gas`.
- An EVM transaction whose fee settlement cannot be computed (an overflow of
  its fee amounts, or a zero base fee) now fails the block with an error
  instead of panicking the kernel. These paths are unreachable for
  well-formed input.
- Converting a fee to EVM gas tells a zero base fee from a gas amount past
  `u64`.
